## 7.2 Response times
- Team messages: answered within 12 hours on working days; blockers within 4 hours.
- Review of a code change: within 12 hours.
- Questions from the Sponsor: answered within 24 hours, by the Product Owner for scope or by the Sprint Leader for process.
- Escalation: a blocker that lasts more than 24 hours is raised with the Sponsor in the next class or by email.
