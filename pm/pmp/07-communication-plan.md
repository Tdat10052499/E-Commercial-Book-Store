## 7.2 Response rules
- Team messages: reply within 12 h on working days; blockers within 4 h.
- PR review: within 12 h.
- Sponsor questions: answered within 24 h by the PO (scope) or Leader (process).
- Escalation: blocker > 24 h → raised to Sponsor in the next class or by email.
