## 5.1 Process
1. **Identify:** risks are raised at the Sprint Planning, at the daily meeting (as blockers) and at the Retrospective; anyone may add a risk.
2. **Assess:** probability from 1 to 5 and impact from 1 to 5; the score is probability × impact. A score of 1 to 6 is low, 8 to 12 is medium, 15 to 25 is high.
3. **Plan the response:** avoid, mitigate (reduce the probability or the impact), transfer or accept; each risk has an owner and a warning sign.
4. **Monitor** in every Sprint, with the status Open, Watching, Occurred or Closed. When a risk occurs it moves to the **Issue Log** with the action taken [03 §6.2-5].
5. **Report** the main risks at each Sprint Review and in the weekly status report.
